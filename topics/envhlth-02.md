## An integrated Bologna contribution

<div class="portfolio-summary">

The portfolio covers the research pathway from **environmental and climate-exposure characterisation** to **human biomonitoring**, **mechanistic investigation**, **multi-omics integration**, **behavioural analysis** and **policy-relevant evidence**.

Individual profiles can be combined to build complementary work packages. Inclusion in this portfolio indicates topic-relevant expertise and interest. It does not imply automatic access to infrastructure or commitment to a consortium.

</div>
